USER GUIDE
  • Area Culling Built-In: Traveling through Stargates or the World Menu Teleport app automatically toggles areas on and off to keep your FPS smooth.
  • Shader Controls: Open the Shaders app in your World Menu to tune Skybox Clouds, Outlines, Optical Stream, Fractal Land, and AudioLink visuals.
  • Per-Area Toggles: Need extra frames? Every major area (Dome, Winter Cabin, Ballpit, World) has individual toggles for rain, snow, grass particles, black holes, and fog — and your preferences persist.
  • Desktop & VR Friendly: You can pick up and dock the World Menu, switch open modes in Menu Settings, and play all mini-games on both VR and Desktop.
